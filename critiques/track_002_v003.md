### C1: Tsumugi's line is a one-bar cell on a loop, with no breath and no phrase arc  [area: melody]  [severity: high]  [confidence: high, listening needed: no]
Observation: In all 27 bars where Tsumugi plays, every note ends inside its bar (0 barline crossings). Bars 4–11 (section a) hold 38 notes and bars 24–31 (a2) hold 37 notes, with **no gap longer than 0.2 beat**. 12 of 27 bars use exactly the onset grid of the `awa` cell (0, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2 beats = 3‑1‑2‑2‑4 sixteenths). b bar 2 (bar 14) reuses it too. 23 of 27 bars end on a one-beat note that starts on beat 3, and then the next bar starts the cell again. a2 bars 0–5 are a verbatim copy of a bars 0–5.
Consequence: The older-sister voice sounds like a music-box cylinder going round (チクタク), not someone humming while she washes hair. The listener never hears a 2- or 4-bar sentence that breathes, asks and answers. That works against naturalness and against ほんわか, which is the most important quality here. Constant 16th motion in the lead also makes the a section feel busier than its 0.5 energy target.
Hypothesis: The melody was written one bar at a time (motif plus a transformation per bar), so the phrase level was never designed. One long note per 2 bars at the half-cadence and cadence points (a bars 3 and 7, a2 bars 3 and 7) plus one variant bar per 4 would create 2+2 phrasing and leave room for Zundamon to answer (see C3).

- `a` bar 7: `"12:6 10:2 9:4"` → `"9:6 r:6"` (hand-over with space before Zundamon's pickup in b bar 0).
- `a` bar 5: motif+transpose → `"10:6 8:2 6:4"` (A5 dotted quarter, F5, D5 over Dm7: the line dips before bar 6 climbs). Keep a2 bar 5 as it is so the reprise still differs from a.
- `a2` bar 7: `"10:6 9:2 8:4"` → `"10:3 9:1 8:8"` (A G F with F held, the same gesture as end bar 2, so the cadence actually lands).
Evaluation: Recount in score.json. Bars with ≥1 rest or held note ≥2 beats should rise from 0/16 to ≥4/16 in a+a2, and exact `awa` grid bars should fall from 12 to ≤9. A/B listening: "Does the A section sound like a hummed phrase (v004) or a looping music box (v003)?"

### C3: Both voices put their long note on beat 3, so the waltz leans forward instead of swaying  [area: rhythm]  [severity: medium]  [confidence: medium, listening needed: yes]

- **High pentatonic bubbles in the gaps** (intro, a, b): audible in their own band and never on top of the melody.
- **The single E6 syncopated climax in a2** (bar 30, beat 2.5, over C7, vel 0.99).
- **No drums, a gentle master** (limiter GR 0.27 dB, crest 16.9 dB, −15 LUFS), the mono low end, the 71 s length, and 96 BPM 3/4 in F major.
